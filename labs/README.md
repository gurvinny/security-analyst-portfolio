## 🎯 Overview
This directory contains comprehensive writeups of attack simulations, CTF challenges, and hands-on lab investigations. The focus is primarily on **defensive security**—analyzing artifacts left behind by attacks, understanding attacker methodology, and developing detection strategies.

---

## 🏗️ Writeup Methodology
To maintain a consistent and professional standard, all lab writeups in this repository strictly adhere to a **5-section structure**:

1. **Executive Summary:** High-level overview of the lab scenario, key objectives, and final outcome.
2. **Alert Triage:** Initial analysis of the triggering alert or the starting point of the investigation.
3. **Investigation & Analysis:** Detailed, step-by-step breakdown of the investigation process, including log analysis, artifact examination, and timeline reconstruction.
4. **Indicators of Compromise (IOCs):** Consolidated list of identified malicious IP addresses, domains, file hashes, and other relevant indicators.
5. **Detection Opportunities & Mitigation:** Recommendations for new SIEM rules, configuration changes, or security controls to prevent or detect similar activity in the future.
