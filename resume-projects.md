## 🏠 Enterprise Home Network Lab
*A fully segmented and monitored lab environment built to simulate an enterprise network architecture.*
*   **Infrastructure Design:** Designed and deployed network segmentation utilizing **pfSense** and VLANs to isolate Lab, Guest, and IoT traffic, reducing the overall attack surface.
*   **Intrusion Detection:** Deployed and configured network-based Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS) such as **Snort/Suricata** to inspect traffic for malicious patterns.
*   **Centralized Logging:** Integrated **Splunk Universal Forwarders** on key endpoints (Windows/Linux) to aggregate critical security events (e.g., authentication failures, process executions) into a central SIEM for proactive monitoring and dashboarding.
*   **Objective:** Established a secure, monitored environment specifically for safe malware analysis, traffic inspection, and detection rule testing.
